Privacy Policy for Tracking Guard

Last updated: July 29, 2026
Privacy Policy Version: 1.1

1. Introduction

Tracking Guard is a browser extension designed to help users identify and manage tracking-related information. We value your privacy and aim to process as little data as possible.

2. Local Processing

The extension performs its main functions locally on your device. Browsing activity, visited websites, URLs, and similar browser data are not collected by us and are not transmitted to external servers.

3. Report Submission

Users may voluntarily submit reports to help improve the extension and fix issues.

Before submitting a report, users are shown this Privacy Policy and must actively agree to it.

When a report is submitted, the following information may be transmitted:

  • Information entered voluntarily by the user in the report form
  • Browser version
  • Operating system information
  • Extension version

Users should avoid entering sensitive or unnecessary personal information into report forms, such as passwords, private keys, or confidential information.

4. Purpose of Processing

Submitted report data is processed for the following purposes:

  • Investigating bugs and technical problems
  • Improving the functionality and reliability of the extension
  • Understanding reported issues

Report data is not used for advertising, profiling, or tracking users.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Submitted reports may be stored for the time necessary to analyze reported issues and improve the extension.

We do not store browsing history or collect information about websites visited by users through report submissions.

6. User Control

Submitting a report is voluntary. Users can use the extension without submitting reports.

Users may choose not to provide additional information in the report form.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the extension or legal requirements.

The current version of this Privacy Policy is always displayed before a user submits a report and must be accepted before any report data is transmitted.
